Research

Self-improving embodied intelligence

Robots that get better from their own experience, needing fewer human demonstrations each time round.

Vision-language-action models

Policies that take in what a robot sees and what it’s told, and answer with motor commands.

World models

Learned simulators that let an agent imagine the outcome of an action before it commits to one.

Before robots, brains. Under the supervision of Prof. Fan Zhang, I worked on deep-learning segmentation of the brain’s white matter, grouping tractography fibres into bundles that agree in both shape and function.
